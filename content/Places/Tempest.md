Founded 100 years ago during [[The Apostasy]]. It was the largest settlement the party had seen at the time it was discussed, though its population is less than one quarter of [[Okros|Okros's]]. 

Fire giants moved here 30 years ago, and [[Haverforth]] was removed from Tempest five years ago.

[[Daxu]] tells [[Kath]] that Tempest only had about 1000 people, but during the [[2026-09-13 Game Notes]], [[Siorce]] informs Daxu that fewer there now.

Tempest is governed by the [[Council of the Elements]], whose current members were elected 25 years ago.

A child from Tempest went missing 10 years ago. Dead bodies were added to the [[Sacred Pyre]] 10 years ago.
