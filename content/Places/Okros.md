A fortified village that has resisted for five decades in the southwestern swamps of the Idol Hollows, and has the brightest [[Sacred Pyre]].

It was a major hub before the Apostasy, and was home to the largest military organization in all of the Halycon Domain. The [[Okros Defense Force]] was a large and well equipped militia/army led by [[Captain Kincaid]].

Okros is considered a village by current standards, but before the Apostasy it was the second-largest city after [[Amber Reach]]. Its population was discussed as roughly 1,500. [[Tempest]] was the largest settlement the party had seen at the time, but has fewer than one quarter as many people as Okros.
