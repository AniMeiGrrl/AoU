A Clank whom the group blames for nearly everything.

## History in Tempest

Haverforth served as a constable in [[Tempest]]. He was forcibly retired and removed from the settlement five years ago after a child went missing. Circumstantial evidence tied him to the disappearance, but his responsibility was never proven.

While serving Tempest, Haverforth convinced the [[Council of the Elements]] to leash an umbral beast to the [[Sacred Pyre]]. Dead bodies were fed to the beast at the pyre. "Feed the fire from the blessed flesh sacrifice."

He was later seen by [[Kath]] in her home grove before it was destroyed.

## What the Tempest Group Believes

The group in Tempest believes that Haverforth is much more than a simple Clank. Their theories and accusations include:

- He may be a veiled god.
- He may work for the Umbra, or otherwise possess a connection to it.
- He is a tool of [[Othedias]].
- He is immortal or otherwise unkillable.
- He is the lone Clank on the Grassy Knoll.
- He was responsible for the missing child in Tempest.
- He caused the blight upon Tempest—or at least knows how to cure it.
- He corrupted the Guardian in the Temple.
- He was looking for children's fables and stories. (which is what led him to Kath's grove)

The group is convinced that children's fables found in the Tempest library contain a hidden message about him.

## What Is Known

Haverforth is **not** the avatar of [[Alu'thir|Alu'thir]].
