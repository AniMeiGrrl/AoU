Alu'thir, the Dawn, is a veiled god of light, healing, mercy, revelation, quiet strength, and protection of the innocent. He is also called **the Last Light**, **the Quiet Flame**, **the Silent Shepherd**, **Lord of the Broken Crown**, and, by some, **the Absent Father**.

His symbol is a broken sun crowned in thorns, with a small flame at its center. His sacred colors are gold fading into pale dawn blue, and white touched with crimson.

## What the Campaign Knows

Alu'thir left the [[Halycon Domain]] 165 years ago. [[Anya the Dawnbringer]] is his avatar; she emerged from the [[Heart of Alu'thir]] when it was planted outside [[Bocindi]]. In the [[2026-09-13 Game Notes|September 13 session]], Anya said he was not on this plane and that he had foreseen the conflict before [[The Apostasy]].

## Stories Told of Him

**The First Silence.** Before there were words, Alu'thir is said to have walked the world listening to every prayer and hurt. He refused to speak divine commands because he believed they would drown out mortal voices. Some call that restraint the Great Mercy; others call it abandonment.

**The Blind Child of Sorrowmere.** During a plague that took sight before life, a silent blind child appeared at a crossroads and drew sickness from the afflicted by touch. When the plague ended, the child vanished into the morning mist. Some say Alu'thir took mortal form; others say the child was merely blessed.

**The Final Blessing.** One tradition says Alu'thir spoke aloud at his departure, urging mortals to carry the flame forward in their own names: “The light was never mine to give, only to reveal.” The account describes him as an old man in torn cloth. It is a sacred story, not an established account of the timing or circumstances of his departure.

## Worship and Practice

Before the Age of Umbra, his temples were described as places of sanctuary, healing, and understanding. They were often built where dawn broke across darkness: east-facing cliffs, storm-lashed islands, and forests regrowing after fire. Tradition names a great temple in Astralyn, the City of First Light, destroyed during a daemon incursion.

His primary clergy, the **Order of the Quiet Flame**, were called **Flamebearers**. They took vows of silence while serving as hospice keepers, midwives, healers, and lantern-lighters. Their practices included:

- **The Vigil of Silence:** Sitting with a candle from dusk until dawn, then extinguishing it at sunrise to honor mortal agency.
- **The Blessing of Broken Things:** Blessing and keeping damaged objects without mending them, as a reminder that brokenness does not erase worth.
- **The Whispering Prayer:** A prayer spoken almost inaudibly, trusting that Alu'thir hears what is too painful or private to say aloud.

## After His Disappearance

Accounts of the **Erasure** say religious and political authorities struck his name from records, reconsecrated temples, and persecuted remaining Flamebearers. These accounts disagree about the motive: some blame rival faiths, while others believe his final blessing threatened those who ruled in the gods' names.

Stories of his surviving influence speak of protective hymns, hidden sacred texts, healing bloodlines, and quiet networks of worshippers. The **Keepers of Dawn** are said to tend concealed shrines; the **Unnamed** continue the Flamebearers' work without invoking his name; and the **Hollow Candles** keep the Vigil in secret. Reported signs of his presence include unexplained light amid suffering, healers who vanish after helping strangers, and flames that refuse to go out.
